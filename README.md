# PaperSage

Scientific editing and manuscript support for biomedical and life-science researchers.

PaperSage helps researchers, PhD scholars and clinician-scientists turn their work into journal-ready manuscripts.

What we offer
- Manuscript writing and development
- Scientific and language editing
- Journal selection and submission support
- Responses to peer-reviewer comments
- Cover letters, abstracts and figure/table checks

Who we help
Biomedical, medical and life-science authors preparing papers, theses and grant documents.

Free resources
- [Biomedical Manuscript Checklist](https://github.com/papersage-org/biomedical-manuscript-checklist)
- [Writing guides on our blog](https://papersage.org/blog)
